# Review 0.57.0.0: ninety nights, and a headline this producer could not read

Version: 1.0 / 2026-09-24

Mandated by D-021 on the major bump. Against `main` at 0.56.0.0 as uploaded to
the session, which is a snapshot of the operator's tree and not a clone of the
remote: nothing below is a reading of GitHub, and nothing below is a reading of
production. `[measured]` means executed in this container; `[reported]` means
taken from the operator's record and not re-measured here.

## 1. What was asked, and what the decision had to move first

The operator asked for the `Bilans nalotu` block to carry the last 7, 30 and 90
days with a chart. D-056 clause 8 had ruled the nights before the last one
internal, and its one-sentence exclusion list named "a series" beside "an
interception rate" and "a figure nobody published". The request therefore could
not be built without amending a decision, and the amendment could not be made
by implication.

D-057 makes it explicitly and draws the line where the protection actually
sits. Arrangement is allowed: their figures, one column per night, side by side.
Argument is not: rates, percentages, moving averages, means per night, trend
lines, projections, and any sentence comparing one window with another. The
justification is that a single night has no scale for a reader on the Polish
side of the border, and withholding the comparison does not prevent one - it
sends the reader to make it where nothing carries provenance.

## 2. The defect the feature found, which is the release's real finding

`strike.headline_total` accepted a total, or a missile figure and a drone
figure to add. A drone-only night carries neither. Over the 91 nights of
`tests/fixtures/kpszsu/summaries.jsonl` the rule found a figure on 55 and
missed it on 35 `[measured]`, so for six weeks `/pl/areas` has printed "Liczby
zestrzelonych nie podano" above a breakdown listing those same drones, on two
nights in five.

It was found by building the series, not by a check: a chart with a third of
its columns empty is unreadable, which is what made anyone look. Logged as
F187. The guard on the repair is the part worth reading - on 2026-07-01 the
headline reads `РАКЕТУ Х-59 ТА 130 ВОРОЖИХ БПЛА`, a missile carrying no number
beside 130 drones, and there the single figure is not the night's total. One
night in 91 takes that branch and it is the one night that must not, so the
branch is refused whenever the headline names an unnumbered class. Coverage
after the repair is 90 of 91 `[measured]`.

## 3. What was built

`EventStore.strike_nights_since` returns one reading per night, the latest,
under the filter `newest_strike_night` already uses, so the tally above the
windows and the last column of the chart cannot disagree. `strike.point`,
`strike.window` and `strike.history` fold those rows into the `strike_history`
key: one point per night across ninety nights, three windows over them, and per
window a sum, the count of nights that went into the sum, a completeness flag
and the peak night with its date. The windows are `report.HISTORY_WINDOWS_DAYS`
imported rather than a second triple.

The block is composed under its own guard in the CLI, beside the night's. A
fold over ninety nights has more ways to fail than a read of one, and the night
is what a reader came for: `[STRIKE-HISTORY-FAILED]` sends this key `null` and
leaves `strike_tally` standing `[measured: a regression driving the failure
through `mavo report --watch`]`.

## 4. Two rules that are the whole of the honesty here

**A window sum ships with its coverage.** Adding across nights is the one new
piece of arithmetic in this release. A sum over 89 of 90 nights presented as
ninety nights is the claim this project exists to refuse, one aggregation up,
so `nights` and `complete` travel with every sum and the page writes `co
najmniej` whenever they disagree.

**A night with no figure is a gap.** `null`, never 0, in the series as in every
other count this producer publishes. A column of zero would say the Air Force
reported nothing shot down that night, which is a claim about the sky; a gap
says we hold no figure, which is a claim about us.

## 5. The anchor, and why it is not a clock hour

A window ending at the newest row in the store would slide backwards with a
dead pipe and keep reporting a full week - F85's shape, one window out. The
window is therefore anchored on the clock. The night in progress has no summary
until morning, and rather than defend a cutoff hour twice a year, the anchor
asks the store: the window ends on today's Kyiv date when a reading for it is
held, and on yesterday's when it is not. A morning summary that never arrives
takes the same test the following day, and the gap appears then.

## 6. What this release does not carry

No consumer change is in this tree; the block that draws the chart is a
`mavo-site` release and lands separately. No host was reached, so the
`Installed` row of `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` still describes the 0.55.3.0 install
and the `Behind by` row states the debt with the command that closes it. The
launched series is published and is sparse by construction - 61 of 91 nights
carry a launched figure `[measured]` - and whether the page draws it at all is
a consumer decision, not one this release takes.
