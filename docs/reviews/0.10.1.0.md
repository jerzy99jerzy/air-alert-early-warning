# Pre-push review, 0.9.2.0 to 0.10.1.0

One review covering five releases, because they were produced in a single
session and pushing them separately would give five reviews written by the same
reader on the same afternoon, which is one review with extra headers.

Releases covered: 0.9.2.0, 0.10.0.0, 0.10.1.0, and the two documentation
releases that preceded them in the same session.

## What changed, in order of consequence

**The thesis was restated and then immediately tested by events.** D-015 made
the tool a reporting instrument rather than a predictor. Two days of measurement
later, the design window turned out to contain 22 western-wide alert episodes
and zero reported Polish airspace violations. Under the old framing that is a
failed sprint; under the new one it is the base-rate argument arriving as an
observation. The decision preceded the measurement by one release, which is luck
and is recorded as luck.

**The channel turned out to be structured.** 99.34% of messages carry a hashtag
naming the area and its unit type. 127 distinct tags in 99 nights, 126 resolving
to a unique state-register code. Two sprints had been planned around the
assumption that F23 needed a bigger vocabulary; it needed someone to read the
message format.

**Sprint 7's engineering landed in an afternoon** and is in the tree:
`mavo/areas.py`, the tag parse wired into the live adapter, unknown tags
reported rather than absorbed, ten regressions.

## Findings raised by this review

**S7 was briefly recorded as shipped, and it is not.** Its exit criterion has
two halves and only the countable half is met: every tag resolves or is marked
unresolved, but the hand-labelled correctness sample does not exist. Marking the
sprint complete on the strength of the half that was easy is precisely the
failure the criteria table was written to prevent. Corrected in this release and
recorded as T36; the sprint stays open.

**A rate was computed on the wrong denominator.** `west_activity` divided
episodes by *active* nights and printed 11.12 per week. The denominator was the
subset in which the phenomenon occurs, which is not a rate any recipient
experiences. Corrected to 5.73 over all 99 nights. Same family as F59, and the
second instance in one session of a restriction that flatters a number.

**A defect entry was itself wrong.** F55 claimed a constant did not exist in the
package; it did, in a module the check had not looked in. Corrected in place as
F56 rather than deleted, because a log whose entries can be wrong is only worth
reading if the wrong ones are marked.

**A probe presented an arbitrary match as an attribution** (F59), and the grep
that exposed it also revealed the structure the whole release rests on. Worth
recording as a pattern: the cheap manual check found in one line what three
releases of reasoning had not.

## What this review did not do

It did not read `mavo/areas.py` against the corpus. The module is tested against
the real 127-row map and against synthetic pages, and the claim that it resolves
*correctly* on real messages is exactly what T36 exists to check. Nothing here
substitutes for that.

It did not verify the register's provenance end to end. The map was built from a
GitHub mirror of KATOTTG dated 2024-01-19; the licence-clean official copy on
data.gov.ua is dated 2021 and the current ministry redaction is from 2026. Three
versions, none simultaneously current and conveniently licensed. `NOTICE` cites
the open-data source as the licence anchor and T31 carries the rest.

## Push readiness

Green: `make verify` OK, 167 tests, coverage 96.97% against a floor of 95, 10 of
11 attacks mutation-verified with the eleventh printed as unverified, docs-audit
pins hold at 0.10.1.0, manual-audit clean.

Outstanding and deliberately unresolved here: T36 (closes S7), T33 (the alias
table), T34 (the 0.66% of untagged messages), T35 (turning the negative result
into a measurement), T6 (legal position, now the most likely cause of a slip
since the engineering path shortened and this one did not).
