# Review 0.60.0.0: the declared level reaches a reader

Version: 1.0 / 2026-10-04

Mandated by D-021 on the minor bump, because the release moves the contract
and a recorded table. Against `main` at 0.59.0.0 (`a59e077`), cloned from
GitHub in the session. **Written by the session that built the release.** It
went through four rounds before it was tagged: two by agent runs given both
trees and a brief but not the session's reasoning; a third by the session
itself, asking what the rule does on the clocks production writes; and a
fourth by an agent run over the third round's repairs, which ran mutants
against them. `[measured]` means executed in the session's container;
`[reported]` means taken from an agent's report and re-run here before acting
on it.

## 1. What was asked

On 2026-10-01 the operator asked to "finally do the change in the alerts from
Ukraine (yellow, red)", and chose in the same exchange: the map fills an
alerted area by the level (the consumer's S-25), producer and consumer ship
together, and the slider shows the level at past moments in the same release.
On 2026-10-04 he asked for a review and a version without errors for
production.

## 2. What was built

`mavo/levels.py`, the one home of the rule, asked by `compose` for the present
and by `timeline.area_layer` for every instant the answer can change. The
contract gains `areas[].level`, `areas[].kinds[].level` and `alert_levels`;
the week gains `level` on every area interval and `areas.levels` (D-063).
`alert_levels` gains `first_read` and `last_read`, written by `collect-api`
before its rows, with the read recorded last, and two indexes. MT18 names the
threat both ways round, and harness A16, A17 and A18 attack it.
`mavo level-reading` takes D-050's reading, the install's first step.

## 3. The first round, and the design it did not save

The first design bounded a declaration by the newest all-clear on its key. The
first round found that two alarms on one key with no poll between them have no
all-clear, so a Red stamped in the first stood beside the second
`[reported, re-run: measured]`; a second bound, the alarm's start less 120 s,
was added. A failed read of the stream cost the whole week; it was guarded.
Both repairs were superseded by the second round.

## 4. The second round: presence, and the documents

- **Three roads to a wrong level, each from the row the bound was drawn
  from** (F196). An alarm re-asserted after a spurious all-clear is dated by
  its observation (D-044): on the week recorded from the production store,
  two of its four re-assertions published no level, for 8 and for 70 minutes,
  while their own rows carried `api_level: Yellow` `[measured on
  tests/fixtures/store_week_2026-09-26.sqlite3.xz, the level stream rebuilt
  from the rows' api_level]`. A channel row on the same key drew the bound
  past the API's own declaration. Two alerts folded onto one key kept the
  ended one's level while the other stood. The rule was rebuilt on presence:
  a declaration stands from its own stamp until a read asked after its
  newest listing has its page in hand.
- **The documents** (F195, F197). D-050's reading, due 2026-09-12, was never
  recorded and nothing asked for it. D-063 ended on a placeholder where the
  reading's figures belonged, a docstring claimed a measurement nobody took, a
  pattern replacement rewrote two historical figures, and prose figures stood
  without their commands. Each repaired against the tree; the history restored
  from `git show a59e077`.

## 5. The third round: the clocks production writes (F198)

The presence rule passed every test, and every test put its reads at one
instant with no fetch time, its all-clears on its reads, and its replay and
level query in one moment. Asked against production's clocks:

- **Major, repaired. A read dated by the moment it asked.** The all-clear a
  read derives is stamped when its page arrived, after the read's
  `started_at`. At the end of every levelled alarm the read in effect no longer
  listed the level while the alarm was still open, so the week carried an
  interval of an open alarm with `level: null`, as long as the fetch. A read
  now speaks from `started_at` plus `elapsed_s`.
- **Major, repaired. A slow page overtaken.** The first repair's agreement test,
  given pages slower than the gap to the next read, found a moment with no read
  in effect, because the index kept two reads before the window. The read in
  effect is now the newest asked among those in hand, and the walk back stops
  at the first read in hand.
- **Major, repaired. Two queries read as one picture.** A collector run
  between a cycle's replay and its level query gave a read whose all-clears
  the replay lacked, and the alarm it ended went out open with no level for a
  cycle. Every command that replays (the loop, the one-shot report,
  `timeline --out`, `timeline --at`) now takes the newest read before its
  replay and uses none after it. The first round's accepted item, the two
  files one poll apart for a cycle, goes with it: the week and the contract of
  a cycle share one horizon.

Every repair was checked by a mutant that undoes it: thirteen, marked 3 in
section 9, each killed by a test that puts the clocks where production does.
A18 entered the harness's register.

## 6. The fourth round: the repairs, run against mutants (F199)

No blocker and no major. Four minor findings and four nits, each re-run here
before it was acted on:

- **Minor, repaired. Two files of one cycle that could disagree.** A failed
  live read published the contract without `alert_levels`, while the week,
  handed the horizon, read the stream on its own; the manual said they could
  not disagree. The week now reads levels only when the picture could, in the
  loop and in the one-shot report.
- **Minor, repaired. A write order asserted by its outcome.** A collector that
  recorded the read before storing its listing passed every test
  `[reported]`. The test now asserts the listing is stored at the moment the
  rows are appended and at the moment the read is recorded.
- **Minor, repaired. A scan on every cycle.** `MIN(first_read)` read the whole
  table: 38 to 41 ms over a synthetic year of 200,000 declarations, against
  under 20 microseconds with `idx_levels_first_read` `[reported, re-run:
  measured]`.
- **Minor, repaired. The rule written four ways**, in F196's repair, the test
  module's docstring, MT18 and a comment in `mavo/levels.py`.
- **Nits, repaired.** `timeline --at` crashed on a failed level read where
  `--out` printed `[LEVELS-FAILED]`. Three behaviours no test pinned: a
  declaration stamped while the horizon's read was in flight, the clamp on a
  negative fetch time, an area's tie going to the later declaration. Two
  sentences of `FEED-SPEC-PL.md`. And the provenance: the reading named by the
  digest of a file outside the tree, the hand mutants in a script outside it,
  T93's sizes against the 0.59.0.0 file, `recorded_since`'s precision unstated,
  and DEPLOYMENT counting three releases behind an `Installed` row its own text
  showed stale.

Listing the mutants for section 9 found one more: the one-shot report's half of
the first repair survived every test, and gained one. The reader, first put
into `tools/`, failed the gate there: an instrument whose input is the store
ships in the package (D-038). It is `mavo level-reading`, whose module imports
nothing from the package, as `mavo/recorder.py` does.
