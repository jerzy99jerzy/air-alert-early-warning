## 2. The question, answered against both trees

**What can move, and what already had.** The consumer computed four things
from sources other than the contract: the RSO communique list and its air-threat
filter, the PAŻP airspace reading, an outage verdict from observation age, and
one slug alias. They do not share an answer.

*RSO and its filter moved.* Two parsers of one feed existed, the producer's
original (`mavo/sources/rso.py`) and the consumer's port of it, and they had
diverged in three ways neither gate could see: page one of one category against
every category unpaged; the doubled autumn hour resolved to the summer offset
against a refusal; a failed refresh keeping names and dropping the communiques
behind them `[measured, both trees]`.

*The PAŻP reader moved.* Not because it was wrong - it is ported rule for rule -
but because it kept one reading in memory, and the operator decided the
scrubber repaints the airspace. The plan cannot be recovered after the fact.

*The outage verdict did not need to move; it needs the consumer to read what is
already here.* This package has published `sources.primary_delivering` since
0.53.0.0. The consumer validates the block and renders from
`observation_age_s > valid_for_s * 6` instead (its D-S69), and
`mavo/liveness.py` records, from the production store, two silences of about
4 900 s on one evening with both pipes at full health `[reported, measured at
0.53.0.0]`. An hour's threshold raises on both. The change is the consumer's.

*The slug alias stays.* `docs/WEBAPP.md` already records that the `kyiv`
mapping belongs on the consumer's side, where the city and the oblast are told
apart, and a renamed slug in the contract would break every other reader of it.

**The handover is self-executing, and that was read rather than assumed.** The
consumer's `_picture` merges its own reading only when the payload lacks the
key, for each key separately `[measured, mavo-site 4.76.0.0 server.py]`. The
producer can ship first, and did not need a coordinated release.

## 3. Findings and their disposition

**R1. Cadences declared, not read.** `mavo/liveness.py` and
`docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` carry 900 s and 300 s for units that do not exist yet.
*Disposition:* stated as declared in both places; the reading is T85.

**R2. Two skies until the consumer's next release.** The text of
`pl_airspace` comes from this package from the first cycle after install; the
consumer's `/airspace.json` keeps serving outlines from its own reading until it
is pointed at `pl_airspace.features`. Both read the same plan minutes apart and
apply the same rules, so a mismatch needs a change of plan inside that window
`[inference]`. *Disposition:* accepted for one consumer release; named in D-053
and in T85.

**R3. A reader-visible change during an RSO outage.** Past an hour without a
read of the map's address the communique block renders *could not read* where
the consumer rendered its last list, empty or not. *Disposition:* intended;
difference 3 in `mavo/poland.py` and in D-053, because the kept list had no
field for its age.

**R4. The unpaged reads are larger than the consumer's.** Five category bodies
per run replace one page; the water-level category held 305 communiques at the
2026-08-22 reading `[reported, STATUS.json]`. At the largest page measured then,
21,418 bytes for 20 items, that category is a few hundred kilobytes per body
`[inference]`, far under the transport's four-million-byte ceiling.
*Disposition:* accepted; the per-run size on the host is owed with T85.

**R5. No retention for three new tables.** *Disposition:* D-054 names the
growth measurement as the input to a policy; T85 takes it.

**R6. The mutations of this release are not registered.** Twelve were run by
hand and all twelve killed after one test was tightened `[measured]`.
`tools/harness_mutation.py` ties each mutation to a threat-model row, and its
`MT` sequence collides with the threat model's own. *Disposition:* accepted as a
debt that predates this release; registering them into a colliding sequence
would deepen it.

**R7. A province without a slug is dropped by this parser and was kept by the
consumer's.** All three provinces in the reduced page recorded from the live
endpoint carry one `[measured, tests/fixtures/rso_page.xml, n=3]`, which says
little about the feed; the first draft of this review said "every province"
and let three rows stand for a population. *Disposition:* accepted
and named in D-053, with its cost stated plainly: `pl_warnings` is organised by
voivodeship, so a communique whose only province lacked a slug would be absent
from the block while its row stayed recorded in `communiques`. That is the
silent shape, and T85's first readings on the host are where it would show.

**R8. The airspace fixture is a stand-in.** It is the consumer's harness file,
which mirrors the schema measured on `vm-site` on 2026-09-14 and is not a
recorded body. *Disposition:* stated in `tests/test_pansa.py`; the first body
read on `vm-mavo` is T85.

**R9. The host table's installed row may already be stale.** `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md`
records 0.54.2.0 installed at 08:02 UTC on 2026-09-10; the handover for this
session reports 0.54.8.0 installed at 09:51 the same day `[reported]`. The
release distance this tree enforces counts from the documented row.
*Disposition:* not changed without a reading; the install ceremony reads the
installed version before anything else.

**R10. The egress check sees quoted addresses under `mavo/sources/` only.** A
destination assembled from parts, or reached from `tools/`, passes it.
*Disposition:* scope stated in the check's docstring; the lint that holds all
reach to `mavo/transport.py` is unchanged and covers `tools/`.

**R11. The install plan's discriminator was not guaranteed by the code it
described.** The plan said the `[STORE-MIGRATED]` lines would be printed by
whichever command opened the store first. Reproduced against the tree: a store
opened first by `mavo report --watch` gained the three tables and printed
nothing, and `mavo rso` printed nothing after it `[measured]`. Five of the
seven store-opening functions at 0.54.8.0 were silent. *Disposition:* F168,
repaired in the second pass of this release: the sentences move to
`mavo.store.migration_lines`, every opener prints them, and a lint in
`tests/lint_domain.py` fails the build on one that does not; red against the
uploaded tree with five names, green here.

**R12. The unit-evidence check is satisfied by a proposal.** The install plan
quotes unit text that no host has served, and
`check_unit_claims_quote_the_unit` accepts it because it reads `[Unit]`
headers and not their provenance. *Disposition:* stated in the plan itself;
the check's own docstring already says it verifies that a claim is sourced and
not that it is true, and a proposal is neither. Not widened here: a check
that distinguished a paste from a plan would have to read prose.

**R13. Three claims in the first draft of this release were written as
measured and were not.** "About 110 MiB a day" was 382,154 × 288 in the wrong
unit, copied from the consumer; "missing from this table from 0.44.0.0" was a
span no revision between had been read for; "every province carries a slug"
was three rows. *Disposition:* each corrected at its site with the arithmetic,
the unread span, or the count beside it; the pattern is F158's, in the release
whose review this is.

**One thing the first draft asserted and the second pass measured.** Putting
the outlines in `state.json` was argued from consistency alone; whether it cost
a reader anything was not asked. It does not: the browser refreshes over
`/fragment/` and `/events`, and the page embeds an airspace summary with no
geometry `[measured, mavo-site 4.76.0.0 render.py, server.py]`. The cost is
the internal push and the server's file read, and D-053 now says so.

**Two defects in the consumer, recorded here because this review found them and
not logged in this repository's register.** `RsoCache.refresh_once` builds the
kept reading without `communiques`, so after one failed refresh every card on
the map loses its text while its voivodeship stays painted; and neither
`RsoCache` nor `rso.voivodeships_now` re-evaluates expiry after a failure, so a
communique that ends during an outage stays painted until the feed answers
`[measured, mavo-site 4.76.0.0 rso.py]`. Both are paid by this move once the
consumer renders the producer's keys. They belong to the consumer's defect
register.
