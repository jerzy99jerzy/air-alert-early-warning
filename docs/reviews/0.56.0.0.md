# Review 0.56.0.0: the strike tally reaches the contract

Version: 1.0 / 2026-09-22

Mandated as the second half of the strike tally, after the operator's
directive of 2026-09-22 to take it to production. Against `main` at `3e50a2f`
(0.55.4.0) as tagged on GitHub, and against the specification of the feature at
edition 0.5, a working file outside the tree. `[measured]` means executed or
read in the container this session; `[reported]` means taken from a handover
or an earlier session and not re-measured here. No host was reached this
session, and nothing below is a reading of production.

## 2. What the specification said, and where the tree disagreed

Four places, each settled in the tree and recorded in D-056's addendum rather
than left to the next reader of the specification.

**The attempt row has no `skipped` column** `[measured]`. Edition 0.5 wrote the
catch-up's remainder "as `skipped` on the attempt row". `feed_attempts` carries
`first_id` and `last_id`, and the alert channel's gaps are derived from them
against the newest `last_id`. The remainder is therefore written as
`skipped_before=<id>` in `detail`, and no column is added to a table with
months of rows in it.

**The reader's `downed_total` adds up items; the contract must not.** The
reader's third branch sums the downed items when every one carries a count,
which is right for checking a reading against itself. On a flagged night it
would publish our reading under their name, which section 3 of the
specification forbids. The contract's `headline_total` reads their total or
their two headline figures and stops `[measured: a unit test, and a mutation
of the flagged rule caught by the contract check]`.

**A flagged night withholds the inventory too.** Edition 0.5 named `downed`
and `launched` as null when flagged and was silent on `launched_total` and
`launched_sum`. The check that flags an inventory cannot tell a misread
inventory from misread items, so both are null; the headline alone stands.

**"The four states of `mavo/poland.py`" lists three.** The fourth, an empty
object, is not a state this key may take: it would render as a night on which
nothing was launched. `contract_check` asserts that a store holding no night
publishes `null`.

## 3. What was found that nobody was looking for

**F186: one threat number named two threats** `[measured]`. The harness had
labelled the all-clear control MT15 while the threat table's MT15 was the
directory lock, and the all-clear had no row. It surfaced only because adding
the strike tally's row meant choosing the next number. Repaired with a row of
its own, MT16, and a check in `tools/harness_mutation.py` that was observed
failing on the old label before it was observed passing on the new one.

**A test of this release asserted a figure nobody had read.** The first draft
of `tests/test_strike.py` claimed post 79268 was fully numbered on its launched
side and its sum therefore publishable. The post names a Banderol after
`а також` with no count, so no total may be derived, and the reader said so:
the test was wrong and the code right. It was caught on the first run, the
recording was read by hand, and the test now pins what the post says, beside a
second one on 66637, which is fully numbered (2, 6 and 142, a total of 150).
That is F185's class in the reviewer's own hand, recorded here rather than
filed, because it never left the session.

## 4. What was verified

The whole gate, `make verify`, on CPython 3.11, 3.12 and 3.14, on the working
tree and on a fresh clone of `3e50a2f` with the patch applied, every touched
file identical byte for byte between the two `[measured]`. The harness register
kills every mutation, including MT17: removing the non-empty condition from
`launched_sum` turns attack A15 red `[measured]`. The command was run end to end
on recorded pages, twice on the same page to see the store refuse the second
write, and the contract read back from the store it wrote `[measured]`.

## 5. What this review does not establish

- That the timer's cadence on the host is 300 s. It is declared and T89 reads
  it.
- That the catch-up behaves on the live channel as on the pages the tests
  build. The walk-back is exercised against a fake transport serving pages of
  the preview's shape; the preview's own paging has been read by the
  operator's harvester and not by this command.
- How a shape of summary the 91 nights do not hold will read. The checks make
  it loud and flag it, and a flagged night publishes their headline alone, so
  the cost of a new shape is a missing breakdown rather than a wrong one.
- Anything a reader of mavo.org.pl sees. That is the consumer's release.
